# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **Board.** A view in the activity bar. At the top, the harbour: a night scene with the lighthouse, its sweeping beam, and the sessions as boats on the water, placed by state and by time in that state; the beam lights the boats it passes and swings to a boat before a jump, and the tide rises with the 5-hour usage. Below, one card per session, grouped by state, showing what each session is doing, its tool calls per minute over the last 15 minutes (fewer while it is younger than that, so the bars grow from the left), how full its context window is, the tokens it has used with their price at API list rates, its project, where it runs and its model. Click a boat or a card to jump; rename a session from its card through the agent's own rename; hide a state group with a click on the legend.
- **Earlier sessions.** Under the live sessions, a collapsed group holds the sessions of the window's folders that are not running and that Claude Code has not archived; a click on the heading opens it, a click on a session resumes it in the agent's panel.
- **Search.** A search box above the cards looks through every conversation, live and earlier, what you wrote and what the agent replied. Sessions with matches stay on the board, each with its newest three matches under the card and the words marked; the rest hide until the box is cleared. Every word must appear in one message. A click on a snippet, or on "+N more", opens the session's whole conversation in a tab, scrolled to the match; the tab has its own search with next and previous, dims what does not match, follows the conversation as it grows, and has a button to bring the session's own tab or terminal forward.
- **Status bar.** Counts of sessions that are working, waiting for you, and finished since you last looked, with a spinner while any session works and a highlighted background while any session waits. The hover list shows every session's title, project, worktree and state.
- **Jump.** `Lookout: Jump to Session` (`Ctrl+Alt+J`) lists the sessions, the ones that need attention first, with how long each has been in its state, and brings the chosen one's tab or terminal to the front. `Lookout: Jump to Next Session That Needs You` (`Ctrl+Alt+Shift+J`) goes straight to the one that has waited longest and cycles through the rest on repeated presses.
- **Finished turns.** A session that finishes a turn while you are not looking at it is marked finished until you open its tab or terminal; the marks survive a window reload. `Lookout: Mark All Sessions as Seen` clears them.
- **Notifications.** A notification with a Jump button when a session starts to wait for you (`lookout.notifications.waiting`, on by default) or finishes a turn (`lookout.notifications.done`, off by default).
- **Usage.** The 5-hour and weekly limits in the status bar item and on the board (bars coloured by consumption: amber from half, red from 80%), from Claude Code's cache. Claude Code fetches them again when they are older than 10 minutes (`lookout.usage.refresh`: `auto`, `manual` or `off`); `Lookout: Refresh Usage` fetches at once.
- **Agents.** Claude Code: sessions in the VS Code panel and in integrated terminals. Sessions in another VS Code window are listed with that window's name.
- **Icon.** The lighthouse is the mark everywhere: the activity bar, and the status bar item through an icon font the extension ships.
- **Platforms.** Linux, WSL, Linux remotes, and Windows.
